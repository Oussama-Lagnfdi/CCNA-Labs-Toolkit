# Lab 1: Basic Network Setup

## Goal
Connect 2 PCs with a switch.

---

## Devices
- 2 PCs
- 1 Switch

---

## Steps
1. Connect PC1 → Switch
2. Connect PC2 → Switch
3. Assign IPs:
   - PC1: 192.168.1.2
   - PC2: 192.168.1.3
4. Ping test:
   ping 192.168.1.3

---

## Result
Successful communication between devices.
